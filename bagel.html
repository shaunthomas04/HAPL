Bagel Recipes

Bagels are a versatile treat, perfect for breakfast, brunch, or a snack. Explore these classic and flavorful recipes that you can make at home.

Classic New York Bagels

Chewy, golden-brown bagels boiled before baking, iconic for their texture.

  • Ingredients: Bread flour, yeast, sugar, salt, water.
  • Method: Mix dough, rise, shape, boil briefly, bake until golden.

Cinnamon Raisin Bagels

Sweet, aromatic, and perfect for cozy breakfasts.

  • Ingredients: Bread flour, yeast, sugar, salt, water, cinnamon, raisins.
  • Method: Fold cinnamon and raisins into dough, shape, boil, bake.

Everything Bagels

Savory bagels loaded with seeds and seasonings.

  • Ingredients: Bread flour, yeast, water, salt, sesame seeds, poppy seeds, garlic, onion flakes.
  • Method: Shape dough, brush with water, coat with seasoning, boil, bake.

Gluten-Free Bagels

Soft and chewy, perfect for dietary restrictions.

  • Ingredients: Gluten-free flour blend, yeast, sugar, salt, xanthan gum, water.
  • Method: Mix, rise, shape, boil carefully, bake until firm.
